How a night works

Every night the CI builds Triton main and runs all AITER Triton benches on it and on the previous night's build, interleaved, 3 repeats each, on the same GPU and AITER tree (AITER main plus the CI's bench additions). Every point more than 5% slower or faster, beyond its own repeat noise, is rerun 5 times and the kernels both builds compiled are compared. Confirmed and assembly changed means an alert (slower) or a credit (faster), and both are bisected to the Triton commit that changed the assembly. Points that are not confirmed, or compile identically, are noise or the machine and do not alert.

Only merged Triton main and AITER main are recorded here. Checks of pull requests and branches run separately and never enter this history.
